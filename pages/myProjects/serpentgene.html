I. Introduction & Showcase

SerpentGene takes the classic grow-your-snake arena formula and layers a perk-driven progression system on top of it. Eating food and defeating enemies grows your body, and every growth step is a chance to draft a new perk that reshapes how you play — weapons, status effects, auras, and passive buffs that stack across your body segments.

Alongside you roam AI-controlled snakes with distinct personalities, each choosing its own path: gathering food, hunting clusters, scavenging corpses, fleeing threats, or setting traps. A dedicated Battle Royale mode turns the arena into a survival match where straying outside the border starts hurting you, and the pressure keeps climbing.

II. Game Systems

1. Game Modes

ModeDescription
Sandbox / PvEOpen arena play against AI snakes, focused on growing, drafting perks and experimenting with builds.
Battle RoyaleSurvival match with a shrinking threat zone. Leaving the border deals escalating damage, so staying inside the arena is part of the fight.

2. Perks & Gacha

Perks are offered through a gacha-style draft as you grow. Each perk has a rarity and can be applied at the body-segment level, so a single perk choice can compound across the whole snake.

SystemDetail
RarityPerks roll at different rarities, which scales their strength.
Body-segment multipliersPerks and auras scale with the number of body segments carrying them.
Weapons / projectilesPerks can grant projectiles with perks of their own, including stacking, chain and boomerang behaviours.
Status effectsBurn, poison, ice-slow and regen, with DOT (damage over time) and self-applied buff variants.
AurasArea effects applied around the snake, including healing and damage-over-time auras.

3. NPC Snakes

AI snakes run a behaviour-based brain with a configurable personality, so different snakes value different things in the arena.

  • Gather & Explore: Locate food hotspots and clusters, and roam the map efficiently.
  • Boost: Spend stamina to chase or reposition when it pays off.
  • Flee & Trap: Detect nearby threats and either escape or bait an opponent.
  • Corpse scavenging: Rush to dropped food after a kill to grow quickly.

4. Mobile Controls

  • On-screen joystick for steering, plus a dedicated boost button.
  • Safe-area handling so the HUD fits notched phones.
  • Touch-friendly menus and in-game panels.

Code Architecture

  • Perk pipeline: A unified damage pipeline applies perks, status effects and hit effects on a single hit path.
  • NPC brain: Behaviours (Gather, Explore, Boost, Flee, Cross, Trap) are selected by a brain that reads a configurable personality and sensory data.
  • Game modes: Mode managers sit on top of the shared arena systems, so Battle Royale reuses the same spawn, NPC and combat code with mode-specific rules.
