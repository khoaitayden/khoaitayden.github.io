SerpentGene

A Slither.io-style arena snake game with roguelike perks, status effects and a Battle Royale mode.

← Back to all projects

I. Introduction

SerpentGene takes the grow-your-snake arena formula and adds a perk-driven progression system. Eating food and defeating enemies grows your body, and each growth step lets you draft a perk — weapons, status effects and auras that stack across your segments.

The arena is filled with AI snakes that each play differently, and a Battle Royale mode where straying outside the border hurts you.

Gameplay Video

II. Features

  • Perks & gacha: Draft perks by rarity as you grow; they scale with your body segments.
  • Combat: Projectile weapons, chain and boomerang behaviours, and burn / poison / slow / regen status effects.
  • AI snakes: Behaviour-based NPCs with personalities — gather, hunt, scavenge, flee or trap.
  • Battle Royale: A survival mode with out-of-border damage.
  • Mobile: Touch joystick, boost button and safe-area HUD.
