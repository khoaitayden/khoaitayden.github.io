I. Introduction & Screenshots

The Fallen One is a personal project created to learn and master Unity development. It blends fast-paced obstacle dodging, creature encounters, multi-phase boss battles, and Tetris-inspired block placement mechanics.

II. Technical & Code Structure

Built in Unity with custom object pooling for creatures and obstacles, state-driven level progression, and dynamic audio mixing.

Code Architecture

  • Player & States: PlayerBehavior.cs, PlayerBehaviorLv2.cs, PlayerController.cs, Bosstage.cs, State.cs, and StateLv2.cs managing player movement, attacks, and boss stage progression.
  • Obstacles & Pooling: Creature2.cs, Creature3.cs, Creature4.cs, Creature5.cs, Obstacle.cs, Ball.cs, and object pool managers (`CreateObstacle.cs`, `ConfigObstacle.cs`, `Creature4PoolManager.cs`).
  • Tetris System: Prefab generation and placement mechanics for Tetris-inspired obstacle grids.
  • Audio Mixer: MasterMixer.mixer controlling master audio layers, sound effects, and ambient music tracks.
