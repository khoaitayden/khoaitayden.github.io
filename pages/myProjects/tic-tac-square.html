I. Introduction & Showcase

Tic Tac Square is a tic-tac-toe with power up (Perk).

The game runs in two modes. PVE is a run-based campaign against an AI opponent: win to process level, draw to stay and lose will decrease level. PVP is local or networked play, first to 5 round wins, with host-configurable turn, highlight and draft timers, a two-press rematch, and a Firebase leaderboard that scores the run.

2. Power Perks (12)

Perks are drafted between rounds and change the board.

PerkEffect
Ghost MarkPlace your mark on an empty cell and a ghost on an adjacent cell. The ghost becomes a solid mark on your next turn.
Horizontal SidePlace your mark, then choose 1 adjacent cell left or right for your 2nd mark.
Vertical SidePlace your mark, then choose 1 adjacent cell above or below for your 2nd mark.
Diagonal SidePlace your mark, then choose 1 adjacent diagonal cell for your 2nd mark.
BlockPlace your mark on an empty cell and a neutral block on any empty cell. Neither side can connect through it.
OverridePlace a solid mark directly over any existing piece (even opponent).
Chaos OnePlace a mark, random mark placed around it in 1×1 radius.
Chaos HorizontalPlace a mark, then a random mark appears anywhere in the same row.
Chaos VerticalPlace a mark, then a random mark appears anywhere in the same column.
Chaos DiagonalPlace a mark, then a random mark appears anywhere on either diagonal.
True ChaosPlace your mark, then a random mark appears in an empty cell.
Chaos GhostPlace your mark, then a random ghost mark appears in an empty cell.

3. Match Rules

  • PVE: Infinite turn time and the AI never receives perks. A win advances you to the next board with no perk offered, a draw keeps you on the level and offers a perk every time, and a loss offers a perk and pushes the run back.
  • PVP: Local or networked, first to 5 round wins (configurable 1–10). After each round the winner drafts the next map, the loser drafts a perk (1 of 3), and a draw gives both a perk. Rematch needs both players to press the button.
  • Host-only match settings: Turn timer 0–60s (default 30s, can be Off), highlight timer 0–30s (default 15s), draft timer 0–30s (default 20s) — the guest's copy reads the rules as the host steps them.
  • Online rooms: Lobbies, waiting room, state sync and rematch run over PurrNet Relay & Lobby, so both peers always agree on the round result and score.
