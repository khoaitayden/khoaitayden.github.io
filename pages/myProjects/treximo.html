I. Introduction

Treximo is a loop-based tactical strategy game developed for GMTK Game Jam 2025 under the theme "LOOP". Players manage inventory items, fight monsters, and survive iterative gameplay loops where positioning and item synergy dictate survival.

Gameplay Video

II. Technical & Code Structure

Built in Unity with a modular scriptable-object asset architecture, combat management, and dynamic difficulty scaling.

Code Architecture

  • Gameplay & Board: PlayerController.cs, BoardManager.cs, and ItemManager.cs handle player actions, item interactions, and grid positioning.
  • Combat System: CombatUI.cs, TestCode.cs, and combat encounter state machines.
  • ScriptableObject Data: Data-driven setup for Items (Axe, Blade, Dagger, Hammer, Shields, Potions) and Monsters (Bat, Ghost, Spider, Witch) with customizable difficulty scaling assets.
  • Audio System: SoundSFXManager.cs managing combat sound effects, mixer states, and audio playback.
